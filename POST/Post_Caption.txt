From sketch to GUI, in one afternoon - a case study in CH5 GUI development with
web development approaches and AI tools.

It started as six boxes and a few arrows in a notebook: an opening screen, rooms,
lights, shades, climate, media.

I sent a photo of the page to an AI collaborator (Claude) with a short brief:
minimalist, black on white, sans serif, a glow that follows the theme.

What came back was a working Crestron CH5 panel. Then the real design work
started - looking at it together and changing it together:

- "Is this intentional?" -> the card outline now fades with the lights
- "Controls in the cards" -> dim and switch a room without entering it
- "The icons draw themselves" -> now on every entry
- "Simulate a live tour" -> a guided demo, built into the panel

Under the hood it is plain web development: hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript,
one CSS variable that flips the glow between black and white, CSS transitions and
SVG line drawing for the motion, Crestron CrComLib for the joins, packed as a .ch5z.

Seven passes later it is "The Showroom", a mockup panel with lighting, shades, climate, a cinema
sequence and a self-running tour.

The sketch and the decisions were mine. The build was fast enough that I could
spend the afternoon on the decisions.

This is a mockup, not a client project - but it changes how early a client can
touch an idea.

- Jassim Alfonso
